Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

What we collect

  • Intake details — the name, email, situation, state, goal, and account descriptions you enter when buying the kit. These personalize your PDF.
  • Payment info — processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Newsletter email — only if you subscribe via the footer form.
  • Basic technical data — standard server logs (IP, user agent) for security and rate limiting.

How we use it

  • To generate and deliver your personalized kit PDF.
  • To email your receipt, access token, and kit.
  • To send occasional template updates — only if you subscribed.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your intake details with third parties except the payment processor (Stripe) and the infrastructure needed to deliver the product.

Your rights

Email info@mehyar.us to request a copy of your data or its deletion. Deleting your intake details does not affect the kit PDF you already downloaded.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. A service worker caches static assets so pages load fast offline.
